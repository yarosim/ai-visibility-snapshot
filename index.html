The problem most small contractors haven’t checked

Your capability statement, SAM and SBA profiles and website were written for humans skimming PDFs. AI engines read differently. They pull from pages that answer a question directly: “FISMA support for Treasury,” “SDVOSB digital services for VA,” “HUBZone cyber teaming partner.”

If those pages belong to other firms, the engines name those firms. That can happen even when you hold the vehicle and the past performance.

From our own dated sample runs (2026-09-24). We ran 5 buyer prompts each for 3 real small federal contractors on a web-search / AI-synthesis layer. The firm was named in 3 of 15 answers. When asked about each firm by name, the engine described all three accurately. The gap was category visibility, not reputation.

That's a small sample on a single engine layer. It shows the kind of gap the Snapshot measures and doesn't predict your result.
