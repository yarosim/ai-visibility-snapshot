FAQ

Who is this for?

Web, SEO and marketing agencies (or freelancers) who maintain websites for local service businesses and want a repeatable way to confirm the site still matches what the client approved.

Do I need to code?

No coding. You fill in JSON templates by replacing placeholders. Running the included checker needs Python 3.10 or newer; it has no other dependencies. You can also use the spreadsheet and forms on their own.

Does the checker crawl live websites?

No. It checks saved HTML pages you download yourself. It does not render JavaScript, schedule checks or send alerts.

What file formats are included?

PDF (playbook, approval form), DOCX (approval form, client summary), XLSX (audit log with dropdowns, summary and review calendar), JSON (5 templates, demo) and the promiseguard.py script.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. Templates are starting points. Facts and wording must come from your client. A REVIEW result means a person should look; it is not proof a claim is wrong.

Can I use it for all my clients?

Yes. License: for use with your own clients; don't resell the pack itself.
