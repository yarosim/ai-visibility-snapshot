A simple client fact-approval process for web agencies

PromiseGuard blog · Oct 31, 2026

When a client says "the website is wrong", the fastest way out is a record of what they approved. Here's a lightweight process any small agency can run.

1. Collect the facts in one place

For each client: business name, phones, address, hours, prices or price ranges, booking URL, service area, current offers, and claims they make ("licensed and insured", "same-day service").

2. Ask what's retired

Ask directly: "Is there anything you used to offer or say that you no longer do?" Retired promises are the easiest errors to miss because nobody is looking for them.

3. Get sign-off with a date

Have the client approve the list and set a review date (quarterly works for most local businesses).

4. Check pages against the list

Required text, forbidden text, exact link destinations. Do it by hand with a checklist, or with a tool.

5. Re-approve when things change

New price? Update the approved list first, then the site.
