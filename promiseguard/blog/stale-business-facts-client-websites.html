7 business facts that go stale on client websites (and how to check them)

PromiseGuard blog · Oct 31, 2026

Most agency websites don't break loudly. They drift. The site loads, the SSL is valid, the uptime monitor is green, and the homepage still says the diagnostic visit costs $99 when the client raised it to $129 last spring.

The seven usual suspects

  1. Phone numbers. Tracking numbers get swapped, offices move, and the old number lives on in a footer or a landing page.
  2. Prices and "starting at" figures. Owners change prices in their head and on invoices long before anyone updates the site.
  3. Hours. Holiday hours, seasonal hours, and "now open Saturdays" banners that were never taken down.
  4. Booking and scheduling links. A new booking tool means a new URL; old buttons keep pointing at the old one.
  5. Service areas. Towns added or dropped as crews change.
  6. Offers and promotions. Expired coupons and "this month only" deals.
  7. Retired promises. "24/7 emergency service", "same-day appointments", "free estimates": claims the business no longer makes.

How to check them

Write each fact down once, get the client to approve it, and check pages against that list: text that must appear, text that must not, and links that must go to an exact place.
