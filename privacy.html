What the forms collect

This site does not have an account or a database. You send only what you type into a form:

  • Ask a question: name, work email, company, and message.
  • Snapshot intake: company, legal name (optional), website, UEI (optional), city and state (optional), main service, second service (optional), primary NAICS, certification, region (optional), main target agency, contract vehicles (optional), up to 3 competitors (optional), the buyer problem you solve (optional), contact name, email, phone (optional), and a checkbox that you agree to be contacted.
  • Free mini-check: name, email, company (optional), and company website.

Each submission also includes the form type and this page’s address. The Snapshot form includes the time you submitted and a JSON copy of the same answers. FormSubmit sends you a short confirmation at the email address you entered.

Where it goes

The form is delivered by FormSubmit.co, which emails the submission to pnsgloballlc@gmail.com. That inbox is how we receive every message about this offer. We use it to reply, and, if you ordered, to prepare your Snapshot. FormSubmit’s own privacy policy is on their site.

If JavaScript is unavailable, the same form posts to FormSubmit and then opens our thank-you page.

What we don’t do with it

We do not sell this information. We do not pass it to advertisers. We do not ask for SAM, email, or website passwords for the Snapshot.

This site does not run analytics or advertising cookies. The form service processes the submission on its servers in order to send the email.

How long it stays

We keep the email so we can answer you and deliver what you asked for. Email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com if you want that message deleted.
