FAQ

Do I have to pay to register in SAM.gov?

No. SAM.gov is free: there is no charge to get a Unique Entity ID, register, or maintain registration. You do not need to pay a third party to complete your registration (SAM.gov; GSA).

How long does SAM.gov registration take?

SAM.gov says to allow at least ten business days after you submit your registration for it to become active. You must renew every 365 days for it to stay active; the kit has you set a reminder at day 330.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. It is an educational guide with templates. Always follow the solicitation and official guidance, and verify every requirement on the official sites.

Where do the facts come from?

Every ground rule cites its source: the SAM.gov Entity Registration Checklist and entity-registration page, FAR 2.101 on acquisition.gov, GSA’s “Register your business” page and SBA size standards.

What exactly do I download?

A zip with the 21-page PDF guide, an Excel workbook (checklist, auto-scoring go/no-go scorecard, compliance matrix, renewal tracker with automatic dates, pipeline and outreach log), Word templates for the capability statement and outreach email, and CSV versions.

I already have a SAM.gov registration. Is this still useful?

Yes, if you have not bid yet: phases 3 to 6 cover positioning, choosing opportunities, compliance and what to do after a bid.

Where can I get free one-to-one help?

Free official help includes the Federal Service Desk (fsd.gov) for SAM.gov, SBA (sba.gov/federal-contracting), APEX Accelerators (apexaccelerators.us) and SBDCs. The kit lists them all.
